Nose in, slow out. Tongue resting on the roof of the mouth, teeth apart, shoulders down. With tones on you can close your eyes: a higher tone means breathe in, a lower one means breathe out. With the glow on, the whole screen fills with colour as you breathe in and fades as you breathe out, so you can follow it without looking straight at it.

If you feel the urge to switch early

  • Breathe smaller. Take in less air than feels natural, so the breath keeps flowing for the whole count.
  • Never park at the top or bottom. Each breath should still be gently moving when the tone changes. Full or empty before the tone means the breath was too big.
  • Quiet, through the nose, into the belly. If someone beside you could hear it, it is too big.
  • Switch early if you need to. Nothing bad happens, and fighting the urge adds the tension you are here to let go. A slower rhythm such as 5 · 7 may also feel easier.
